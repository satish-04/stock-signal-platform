# Risk checklist — run before writing any conclusion

- [ ] **Low-quality or anonymous sources** — how many posts come from accounts with a real name, track record, or stated role?
- [ ] **Coordinated posting and bots** — same wording, same minute, new accounts? Treat as one voice at most, or zero.
- [ ] **Duplicate posts** — reposts and copies removed before counting?
- [ ] **Rumors presented as facts** — does the post cite anything outside X? If not, it's a rumor until you verify it.
- [ ] **Sarcasm and misclassified sentiment** — did you re-read every low-confidence label yourself?
- [ ] **Small sample sizes** — 15 posts is a conversation, not a market. Say so in the thesis.
- [ ] **Selection bias** — did your search terms or your own views pick the posts? Did you include ones you disagree with?
- [ ] **Missing market context** — do you have the price, volume, and news timeline from outside X?
- [ ] **Sentiment changing quickly** — is your data older than a day or two? Date-stamp everything.
- [ ] **Correlation is not causation** — posts that line up with a price move didn't necessarily cause it (or predict the next one).
- [ ] **Independent verification** — every claim you'd act on checked against a filing, transcript, or primary source?
- [ ] **Human approval** — did a human (you) read the full thesis and decide? No output from this system is a decision.
